       (g)    The past experience of the applicant in the matter to which the permit appertains

proposed operations.

       (h)     The location of the place and the name and address of all persons to whom any
materials from the excavation are to be sold or delivered.

       (i)    Whether or not any permit of the applicant has been revoked and if so the
circumstances of such revocation.

       (j)     The time within which such excavation is to be commenced after the granting of
the permit and the time when it is to be completed.

      (k)     Such further information as the city engineer, superintendent of streets or the City
Council may require. (Ord. No. 110 N.S., '2)
