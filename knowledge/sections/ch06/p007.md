     The appeal must be in writing; specify the reasons for the appeal; contain your name,
     address, and telephone number; be accompanied by an appeal fee of
     _____________________ dollars ($____________); and be submitted to the City Clerk
     at the following address:

            City Clerk
            City of Piedmont
            120 Vista Avenue
            Piedmont, CA 94611

     One who is legally indigent may apply for a waiver of the appeal fee. Upon timely

     matter to be set for hearing and notify you of the date and location of the hearing.
