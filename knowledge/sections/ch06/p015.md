       3.     Posting and Publishing. Where the owner’s address is unknown, service may be
              accomplished by posting and publication. Posting of the notice and order shall be
              conspicuously on or in front of the property. If the property has no frontage,
              posting shall be on the portion of the property nearest to a street, highway, or
              road, or most likely to give actual notice to the owner. Within five (5) days of
              posting the notice on or in front of the property, the notice shall be published in a
              newspaper of general circulation within the City of Piedmont. The failure of a
              person to receive a properly addressed service shall not affect the validity of the
              proceedings. Service is deemed complete on the day the notice is posted on or in
              front of the property. Where a notice and order to abate is served by posting and
              publishing, the issuance date listed in the notice and order to abate shall be the
              date the notice is published. (Ord. 518 N.S., 7/1990; Ord. 735 N.S., 10/2017)
