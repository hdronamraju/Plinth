# Piedmont City Code §13.05.030 Removal of cart

The city may request a sidewalk vendor remove any cart that is in violation of this chapter. If the
sidewalk vendor refuses to remove the cart, or if a cart has been abandoned, the city may cause
the cart to be removed and may, after adequate notice, subsequently dispose of the cart
(including any associated merchandise and food) if not claimed by the vendor in accordance with
the rules and regulations.
