# Piedmont City Code §13.02.010 Purpose

The purpose of this chapter is to establish a sidewalk vending program within the city while
maintaining objective regulations that are directly related to public health, safety, and welfare.
This chapter shall also regulate solicitation within in the city in a manner to protect public health,
safety, and welfare.
