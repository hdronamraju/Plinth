# Piedmont City Code §13.05.010 Administration

A. The police department is authorized to issue vending permits in accordance with this
chapter.

B. The Police Chief is authorized to develop, and enforce, the rules and regulations regarding
the licensing, permitting, and operation of sidewalk vending, in accordance with this chapter.
