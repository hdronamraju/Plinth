SEC. 2.155    PURCHASES FOR $5,000.00 OR LESS

Purchases of services, supplies or equipment for $5,000 or less and for which an
appropriation is included in the Adopted Budget, shall be approved by individual Department
Head requiring the services, supplies, or equipment. Additional purchasing procedures shall
be established by City’s Administrative Purchasing Policy. (Ord. No. 716 N.S., 02/15, Ord.
727 N.S., 4/17)
