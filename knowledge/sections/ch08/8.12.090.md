# Piedmont City Code §8.12.090 Adoption of EBMUD Regional PSL Ordinance

The EBMUD Regional PSL Ordinance, is hereby adopted by reference. One copy of the
EBMUD Regional PSL Ordinance shall be kept on file at the Department of Public
Works.
