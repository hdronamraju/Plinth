# Piedmont City Code §8.14.040 Designation of Very High Fire Hazard Severity Zone

The City Council designates those areas identified on the City’s Very High Fire Hazard
Severity map as designated in figure 8.14.040, as Very High Fire Severity Zones in
accordance with section 51179(a) of the California Government Code. A copy of such
map shall be kept on file with the Fire Marshal. (Ord. 755 N.S., 08/2020)
