# Piedmont City Code §8.12.060 Abandonment of existing building sewers

An existing building sewer lateral, or its connection, which is to be abandoned must be
removed or sealed with a permanent, watertight plug at the connection to the public
sewer in a manner satisfactory to the Director. All other openings of the abandoned
building sewer lateral including plumbing connections, clean outs, rat holes, etc. must be
similarly sealed.
