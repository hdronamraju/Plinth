§17.90.010 Bedroom includes any room with features generally characteristic of bedrooms,
regardless of its designation on a building plan. A bedroom has adequate privacy and meets the
minimum size and habitation requirements of the Building Code. It includes and is not limited to
a room with: (a) access to a full bathroom on the same floor or within half a floor, if the house has
a split level; (b) access to a full bathroom through a common hallway or other common space such
as a kitchen, living room and/or dining room. A bedroom need not have a closet.
