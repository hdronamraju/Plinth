design of the project conforms to all the standards listed below (§17.66.060). Please describe how the proposed
project meets the standards for approving a design review permit:

area openings, breaks in the façade, line and pitch of the roof, materials, arrangements of structures on the parcel,
and concealment of mechanical and electrical equipment. You may cite specific guidelines and General Plan
