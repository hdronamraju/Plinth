   How to apply
   The City of Piedmont has a new online permitting system called eTRAKiT . You can now apply for and
   track your design review planning permit online. Paper and email applications for planning permits are
   no longer accepted.

   Before you apply, we recommend you contact us to discuss your application. This will often lead to a
   faster turnaround time.
