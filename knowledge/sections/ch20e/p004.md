# Chapter 20E: Special Municipal Sewer Tax, Page 4

SECTION 20E.8 DURATION, AMENDMENT OR REPEAL

        This Ordinance, or any provision thereof, may only be amended or repealed by
approval of a two-thirds majority of the voters voting on the proposition at any initiative
or referendum election.

        This Ordinance shall be adopted, if approved by a two-thirds majority of the
voters voting on the proposition at an election to be held on Tuesday, March 7, 2000, and
shall be effective commencing with the fiscal year that begins on July 1, 2001; provided
that the provisions of Chapter 20B in effect on March 7, 2000 shall remain in effect for
all purposes relating to the taxes provided thereunder through the fiscal year ending June
30, 2001.

SECTION 20E.9 EXEMPTIONS

         The City Council may from time to time by Council resolution adopt exemptions
to the tax measures set further herein.
