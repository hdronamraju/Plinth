# Piedmont City Code §9.04.020 Waste management plan

To ensure compliance with the diversion requirement in this division, each applicant shall submit
a completed waste management plan in a manner and form determined by the building official
prior to issuance of a city building permit. The building official shall approve the waste
management plan only if it will meet the diversion requirements of this division. To further
secure compliance, the building official may require an applicant to deposit a performance
security in an amount not exceeding two percent of the anticipated total project cost. Such
security may be provided in the form of a cash deposit, money order or alternate security
approved by the city attorney. Compliance with this section is not required where the building
official has determined that an emergency demolition is required to protect the public health and
safety.
