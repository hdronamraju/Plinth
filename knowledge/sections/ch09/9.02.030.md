# Piedmont City Code §9.02.030 Collection of recyclable materials by unauthorized persons

From time of placement of recyclable materials in a Recycling Container, those recyclable
materials become the property of the city or its contractor. Unless otherwise authorized by this
chapter, it is a violation for any person other than the contractor to collect or pick up or cause to
be collected or picked up any such items.
