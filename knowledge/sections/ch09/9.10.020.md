# Piedmont City Code §9.10.020 Container removal fine

The city will provide written notification to single-family dwelling service recipients who violate
Section 9.04.050. For the fourth and subsequent violation of this section in any one calendar
year, the city may impose fines as follows:
        Forth offense $50.00 fine
        Fifth offense $100.00 fine
        Six offense $150.00 fine

The failure to pay fines imposed, or the seventh offense for failure to comply with the
requirements of Section 9.04.050, constitutes a violation of this chapter. If a violation has
occurred, the director may require that the single-family dwelling service recipient subscribe to
and pay for on-premises collection as provided by the contractor.
