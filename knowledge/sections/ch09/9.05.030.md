# Piedmont City Code §9.05.030 Collection vehicles weight restriction

A vehicle removing or carrying solid waste or construction and demolition debris may not
exceed 18,000 pounds per axle, and may not exceed 52,000 pounds gross vehicle weight when
loaded.
