# Piedmont City Code §9.10.010 Enforcement Authority

The director, or his or her designee, has the authority to enforce this chapter and the WMA
ORRO, in addition to the authority granted to police officers. The director is authorized to
establish rules and regulations for the purpose of enforcing of the provisions of this chapter and
the WMA ORRO.
