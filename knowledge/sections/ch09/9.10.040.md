# Piedmont City Code §9.10.040 Enforcement of Organic Waste

Any violations relating to organic waste shall be enforced by an Enforcement Agency or the
City of Piedmont in accordance with the provisions of Section 12 of the WMA ORRO.

(Ord. 763 N.S. 01/2022)
