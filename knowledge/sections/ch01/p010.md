SEC. 1.12    AMOUNT OF FINES

A.    The amounts of the fines for code violations imposed pursuant to this article shall be set
      forth in the schedule of fines established by resolution of the City Council.

B.    The schedule of fines may specify any increased fines for repeat violations of the same
      code provision by the same person within a specified monthly period from the date of an
      administrative citation.
