SEC. 11.81     PARKING DISTRICT REQUIREMENTS

         If a parking district is created under Section 11.80 of this Code, no car shall be parked on
any street in the district between the hours specified by the City Council except that the Police
Chief or his designee may issue on-street parking permits to residents living in the parking
district and their guests. (Ord. No. 516 N.S., 4/90, Ord. No. 618 N.S. 10/00)

SEC. 11.82     VIOLATIONS

        A violation of any provision of this ordinance is deemed an infraction and is punishable
by a fine as may be established by the City Council from time to time in the City Parking Penalty
Schedule.(Ord. No. 516 N.S., 4/90, Ord. No. 618 N.S. 10/00)

SEC. 11.83     ARTUNA AVENUE PREFERENTIAL PARKING

           The City Council shall adopt a preferential parking system for the Artuna Avenue
residents to mitigate traffic and parking related to creation of Dracena Quarry Park. (Ord. No.
566 N.S., 5/95)

SEC. 11.84     PERMIT PARKING IN FRONT OF PRIVATE DRIVEWAYS.

       Within districts which have been previously designated by the City Council as "Parking
Impacted Districts," the Police Chief may issue at the discretion of the Police Chief a permit to a
Piedmont resident to park in front of that resident's private driveway; provided that under no
circumstances shall this authorize parking on a City sidewalk. (Ord. 632 N.S. 8/02)
