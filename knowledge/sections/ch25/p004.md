# Chapter 25: Commissions, Page 25-4 (PDF page 4)

                     The City Council may direct City staff members to attend meetings of a particular
      commission either on a regular or special basis to assist that commission in its work. (Ord. No.
      395 N.S., §15)

      SEC. 25.13    REPORTS

                      Annually, on or before the first day of May, each commission shall submit in
      writing to the City Council a report assessing those programs and activities concerning its
      respective areas of responsibility, including the submission of programmatic and fiscal
      recommendations as appropriate. Each body shall also be responsible for submitting such
      additional reports as necessary or as requested by the Council. (Ord. No. 395 N.S., §16)

      SEC. 25.14    ABSOLUTE MAJORITY VOTE REQUIRED

                      Notwithstanding any other provisions of this Chapter 25, except for actions which
      are strictly advisory in nature, any action taken by a commission shall not be valid or binding
      unless adopted by an affirmative vote of an absolute majority of the commission members, such
      absolute majority consisting of the smallest number of members constituting in excess of fifty
      (50%) percent of the total membership of that commission. (Ord. No. 429 N.S., §2)
