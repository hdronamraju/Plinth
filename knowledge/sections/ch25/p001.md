      SEC. 25.1      REGULATIONS GOVERNING COMMISSIONS

                      All commissions shall act in conformance with Article VI of the City Charter,
      which this Chapter 25 is intended to implement. Unless otherwise indicated, the regulations set
      forth in this Chapter 25 of the City Code shall govern all commissions which are created under
      this Chapter 25. (Ord. No. 395 N.S., §2)

      SEC. 25.2      PLANNING COMMISSION COMPOSITION; REGULAR MEETINGS

                  There is hereby established a planning commission consisting of five (5)
      members. The commission shall meet on the second Monday of each month. (Ord. No. 395
      N.S., §3)
