SEC. 27.9      FEE EXEMPTIONS

       The United States Government, the State of California, counties, municipal corporations,
departments thereof and other governmental entities are exempt from all fees authorized by this
chapter.

SEC. 27.10     CAR ALARMS

      The provisions of this Chapter are not applicable to audible alarms affixed to
automobiles. (Ord. No. 511 N.S., 7/89)

SEC. 27.11     CONFIDENTIALITY

        The information furnished and secured pursuant to this ordinance shall be confidential in
character and shall not be subject to public inspection and shall be kept so that the contents
thereof shall not be known except to the persons charged with the administration of this
ordinance. (Ord. No. 511 N.S., 7/89)
