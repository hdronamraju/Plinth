# Corner Obstructions Regulations, Page 1

CORNER OBSTRUCTIONS
[Transcribed by hand from a scanned, hand-drawn diagram. The PDF has no text layer.]

No hedges, fences or walls can exceed 3' in height in this area of the property, without Planning Commission approval.

The diagram shows a corner property. The shaded area is at the street corner. It is measured 30' along each street side from the "extended intersection", the point where the imaginary extension of the property lines meet. The shaded area is bounded by the property lines at the corner and a straight line joining the two 30' points.

Labels in the diagram: 30' (shown twice), Curb, Sidewalk, Extended intersection, Imaginary extension of property lines, Corner property, Property line.
