# Chapter 4A: Bicycles, Page 5

SEC. 4A.5      EXAMINATION OF APPLICANTS

        Upon making an application for his first license, registration card and license plates or
insignia, the applicant may be required to be examined by an examining officer designated by the
chief of police. The chief of police may further designate the manner of examination. Upon
completion of the examination, the chief of police may refuse to issue a license to the applicant if
the examination discloses to the chief of police that the applicant does not have a reasonable
knowledge of the provisions of this chapter, the provisions of Section 21050 of the Vehicle Code
of the State, or that the applicant is unable to exercise reasonable control in operating a bicycle.
(Ord. No. 213 N.S., '6)

SEC. 4A.6      LICENSE-FEE

         Every person desiring a bicycle license shall pay to the chief of police a fee of one dollar
per year payable in advance at the time the application is made. The license may be issued up to
a total period of three years at the discretion of the chief of police, for a maximum fee of three
dollars for three years, and the fee may be prorated on a reasonable basis for the actual period
issued at the discretion of the chief of police. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '5; Ord. No. 360 N.S., '1)

SEC. 4A.7      LICENSE-APPLICATION; REQUIREMENTS TO BE MET BEFORE
               ISSUANCE

        Every person desiring a bicycle license in the City shall make application to the chief of
police Upon blanks furnished by him. Such application must be filed in duplicate and shall
contain such information as may be required by the chief of police as to the applicant and the
description of the bicycle licensed. No license shall be issued unless the bicycle to be licensed
complies with the requirements of this chapter as to its safe mechanical condition, and has affixed
to the rear fender and, if there is no rear fender, to a permanent part of the frame, in a position
readily visible from three hundred feet from the rear of such bicycle a red lamp, a red reflector or
a piece of red reflectorized tape at least two inches in length. (Ord. No. 213, N.S., '4; Ord. No.
295 N.S., '1)

SEC. 4A.8      LICENSE-EXPIRATION

        All bicycle licenses shall expire on the thirty first day of December of the last year of the
period for which they are issued. (Ord No. 213 N.S., '7; Ord. No. 360 N.S., '2)
