# Chapter 4A: Bicycles, Page 6

SEC. 4A.9      LICENSE-RENEWAL

         Application for renewal of a bicycle license expiring on the date mentioned in section
4A.8 shall be made by the owner, between January first and March first succeeding the
expiration date, and shall be made by presentation of the license plate and the registration card
last issued for the bicycle, and by the payment of the fee for the bicycle in the same manner as
provided in section 4A.6. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '8; Ord. No. 360 N.S., '3)

SEC. 4A.10     LICENSE-PENALTY FOR FAILURE TO RENEW; EXCEPTIONAL CASES

        A penalty shall be imposed Upon the bicycle owner who fails to comply with section
4A.9 in the amount of one dollar; provided, however, no penalty shall be imposed Upon the
owner of a newly purchased bicycle who obtains a license within fourteen days of the purchase;
or to a new resident of the City who obtains a license within thirty days of becoming a resident
of the City; or, when the owner can provide adequate proof that he was unable to operate the
bicycle during the renewal period due to mechanical failure. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '9, Ord. No.
360 N.S., '4)

SEC. 4A.11     ISSUANCE OF PLATE AND REGISTRATION CARD UPON APPROVAL
               OF APPLICATION; EFFECTIVE DATE

         Upon approval of the application, and payment of the license fee, the chief of police shall
issue a bicycle license plate and registration card which shall be effective until December thirty-
first of the last year of the license. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '7; Ord. No. 360 N.S., '5)

SEC. 4A.12     DESIGNATION OF SIZE, FORM, ETC., OF PLATE AND CARD TO BE BY
               CHIEF OF POLICE; PLATE TO BE AFFIXED TO BICYCLE; CARD TO BE
               CARRIED BY LICENSEE

       The size, form and character of the license plate or insignia and registration card shall be
designated by the chief of police. The license plate or insignia shall be affixed to the bicycle in
the manner designated by the chief of police. The registration card shall be carried by the licensed
person. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '10)
