# Chapter 4A: Bicycles, Page 9

SEC. 4A.25     RIDING ON SIDEWALK

        No person shall ride or operate a bicycle on any sidewalk in the City, except as herein
specifically permitted. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.26     JUVENILES RIDING ON SIDEWALK

        Juveniles under the age of sixteen years, exercising due care and giving pedestrians the
right of way, may ride and operate their bicycles Upon the sidewalk, except such sidewalks as
are in front of schools, stores or buildings used for business purposes. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.27     KEEP TO RIGHT OF ROADWAY

        Persons riding or operating bicycles on a highway or street shall keep such bicycles as
close to the right hand curb as possible, except when preparing to make a left hand turn. (Ord.
No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.28     RIDING IN A GROUP

        Persons riding or operating bicycles in the City shall not ride more than two abreast,
except on paths or parts of a roadway set aside for the exclusive use of bicycles; provided,
further, that persons riding bicycles on the sidewalk shall do so in single file. (Ord. No. 213 N.S.,
'15)

SEC. 4A.29     CLINGING TO MOVING VEHICLES

       No person riding or operating a bicycle in the City shall cling or attach himself or his
bicycle to any other moving vehicle or streetcar, or person in any other vehicle. (Ord. No. 213
N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.30     PASSENGERS

bicycle, unless such person or passenger is seated Upon an individual seat or carrier separate
from that intended to be used by the operator.
