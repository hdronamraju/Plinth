# Chapter 4A: Bicycles, Page 10

N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.31     TOWING

        No person riding or operating a bicycle in the City shall tow any other vehicle or person,
except that bicycle trailers used for the delivery of newspapers, magazines or merchandise may
be towed when being used in such delivery service. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.32     CARRYING ARTICLES

        No person riding or operating a bicycle in the City shall carry any package, bundle or
article which prohibits the rider from having full control of the bicycle at all times. (Ord. No. 213
N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.33     RACING

        No person riding or operating a bicycle Upon a public highway or street shall participate
in any race, speed or endurance contest unless such race or endurance contest has the written
permission of the chief of police, and under his supervision. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.34     TRICK RIDING

       No person riding or operating a bicycle shall perform or attempt to perform any
acrobatic, fancy or stunt riding Upon any public highway or street in the City. (Ord. No. 213
N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.35     TRAFFIC SIGNALS

        Every person operating a bicycle in the City shall stop for all arterial highways and traffic
signals. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.36     PARKING

        No person shall park any bicycle against windows or parking meters or on the main-
traveled portion of the sidewalk, nor in such manner as to constitute a hazard to pedestrians,
traffic or property.
