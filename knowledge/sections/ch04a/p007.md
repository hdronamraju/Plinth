SEC. 4A.14     PROCEDURE UPON LOSS OF REGISTRATION CARD

       Upon the loss of a registration card, the licensee shall make application for a duplicate
card which shall be issued to him free of cost. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '12)

              ARTICLE III. MECHANICAL CONDITIONS AND EQUIPMENT

SEC. 4A.15     GENERALLY

        Every person desiring a bicycle license must demonstrate to the chief of police that the
bicycle for which the applicant desires to secure license plates or insignia meets the requirements
as to safe mechanical conditions set out in this article. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '13)

SEC. 4A.16     BRAKES

       Every bicycle shall be equipped with a brake adequate to skid the front or rear tire of the
bicycle Upon application of the brake. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '13)

SEC. 4A.17     WARNING DEVICES

       Every bicycle shall be equipped with a horn or bell in good working order, capable of
emitting sound audible for a distance of not less than one hundred feet under normal conditions.
(Ord. No. 213 N.S., '13)

SEC. 4A.18     SIRENS AND WHISTLES

       It shall be unlawful to equip a bicycle with a siren or whistle. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '13)

SEC. 4A.19     HANDLEBAR GRIPS

       Every bicycle that is equipped with handlebar grips must have such grips securely glued
or cemented to the handlebars. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '13)
