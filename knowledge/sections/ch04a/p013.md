# Chapter 4A: Bicycles, Page 13

       Every person signalling a left turn shall do so by extending his left hand and arm
horizontally beyond the side of the bicycle. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.48     SAME-RIGHT TURN

      Every person signalling a right turn shall do so by extending his left hand and arm upward
beyond the side of the bicycle. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.49     SAME-STOP OR SUDDEN DECREASE OF SPEED

      Every person signalling a stop shall do so by extending his left hand and arm downward
beyond the side of the bicycle. (Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.50     PROCEDURE IN EVENT OF ACCIDENT

        The operator of any bicycle involved in an accident shall take reasonable steps to
ascertain whether or not anyone was injured, and he shall give his name, address and the license
number of his bicycle to the person with whom he was in collision; and he shall obtain the same
information from the other person.

        It shall be the duty of the bicycle operator to make a written report of any accident
resulting in death or injury to the police department within twenty-four hours of such accident.
(Ord. No. 213 N.S., '15)

SEC. 4A.51     RESTRICT BICYCLES AND OTHER WHEELED VEHICLES TO
               PATHWAYS IN PARK

       No bicycle or other wheeled vehicle shall be allowed in any part of a City park, except
Upon paved pathways or sidewalks within such park. This section does not apply to City
operated vehicles, babies in strollers or carriages, handicapped persons in wheelchairs, and such
other vehicles as are exempted from me to time by the city administrator. (Ord No. 394 N.S.,
'1)
