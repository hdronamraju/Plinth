        12.5.1 Fortune-Telling, Etc. for Fees, Salary or Any Other Compensation. It shall be
unlawful for any person to exhibit or conduct within the corporate limits of the City, for fees,
salary or any other compensation, the business, art or practice of astrology, palmistry,
phrenology, life reading, fortune-telling, cartomacy, clairvoyance, clairaudience, crystal gazing,
mediumship, prophecy, augury, divination, magic or necromancy. (Ord. No. 234 N.S., 12/62)

       12.5.2 Insulting, Annoying, Etc., Remarks to or Molestation of Another in Public.
       It shall be unlawful for any person to make audible annoying or insulting remarks to or
about or to molest in any other manner whatever, any person in, near or about a public place.
(Ord. No. 237 N.S., 4/63)

SEC. 12.6      INTOXICATED PERSONS-PROHIBITED FROM BEING IN OR ABOUT
               ANY VEHICLE

        It shall be unlawful for any person who is under the influence of intoxicating liquor or
narcotic drugs to be in or about any vehicle to which he has right of access or control while such
vehicle is in or upon any street or any other public place in the City, unless the vehicle is under
the immediate control or operation or a person not under the influence of intoxicating liquor or
narcotic drugs. (Ord. No. 163 N.S., 10/53)

SEC. 12.7      SAME-UNLAWFUL TO APPEAR IN PUBLIC PLACE

       It shall be unlawful for any person to appear in any public place, or place open to public
view in the City, in an intoxicated or drunken condition. (Ord. No. 162 N.S., 10/53)

SEC. 12.8      NOISE DECLARED NUISANCE

        Notwithstanding any other provision of this Code, and in addition thereto, it is unlawful
for any person to willfully make or continue or cause to be made or continued or to allow any
animal to make or continue to make any loud, unnecessary, or unusual noise which disturbs the
peace and quiet of any neighborhood or which causes discomfort or annoyance to any reasonable
person of normal sensitivity residing in the area. Such noise is declared to be a nuisance.
