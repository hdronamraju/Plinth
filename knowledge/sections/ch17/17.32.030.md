# Piedmont City Code §17.32.030 Corner obstructions

No fence, wall, retaining wall, hedge, or vegetation may be erected or grown in the front or street
side yard of a corner lot to a height of more than three feet within the sight distance zone, a
triangular area formed by measuring 30 feet along the front and side lot lines along the right-of-
way from their “extended” intersection and connecting these two points, or as otherwise may be
approved by the Planning Commission.
