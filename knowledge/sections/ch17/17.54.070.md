# Piedmont City Code §17.54.070 Accessory Dwelling Units

Accessory dwelling units (ADU) and junior accessory dwelling units (JADU) are permitted on a
parcel that has undergone an SB9 urban lot split or is a two-unit housing development that
conforms with California Government Code Sections 65852.2 (ADUs) and 65852.22 (JADUs).
Once a parcel has been divided pursuant to the urban lot split provisions, the maximum number of
dwellings on each resulting parcel, inclusive of any ADUs or JADUs, is two. On a parcel with a
two-unit housing development, the maximum number of units allowed is four (inclusive of two
primary units, an ADU, and a JADU).
