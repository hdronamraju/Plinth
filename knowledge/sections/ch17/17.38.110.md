# Piedmont City Code §17.38.110 Appeals

The Applicant may only appeal a determination that: (1) an application of an accessory dwelling
unit or junior accessory dwelling unit is incomplete, or (2) an application of an accessory
dwelling unit or junior accessory dwelling unit is denied.

A. Submission: The appeal shall be submitted in writing to the Director within 5 calendar days
   of the decision, stating the grounds for appeal. The request must be timely filed with the City
   Clerk, and accompanied by the appeal fee, which fee shall be established by Resolution of
   the City Council.

B. Hearing Body: The appeal will be heard by the Planning Commission, and review shall be
   conducted in accordance with Section 17.78.040. The decision of the Planning Commission
   is final and not subject to further appeals.

C. Timing: A final written decision on the appeal shall be issued within 60 days of the City’s
   receipt of the written appeal request and payment of the appeal fee. (Ord. 789 N.S., 03/2026)
