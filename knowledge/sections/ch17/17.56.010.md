# Piedmont City Code §17.56.010 Purpose and intent

The purpose and intent of this division is to establish standards for emergency shelters that
ensure compatibility of shelter activities with surrounding uses and provide a safe place for
individuals and families to obtain temporary shelters, in compliance with state housing law,
including Government Code section 65583.
