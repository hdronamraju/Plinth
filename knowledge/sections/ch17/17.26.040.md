# Piedmont City Code §17.26.040 Prohibited uses

The following uses are prohibited uses in Zone D: manufacturing, wholesaling, distributing, or
industrial use; motor vehicle sales or service (except as provided in section 17.26.030.D); hotel
or motel; fast food restaurants; and drive-through establishments. (Ord. 783 N.S. 10/2025)
