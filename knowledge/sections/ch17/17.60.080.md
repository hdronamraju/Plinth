# Piedmont City Code §17.60.080 Withdrawal of application

An applicant may withdraw an application at any time during the review process before the final
decision, by filling out the request for withdrawal form. Upon withdrawal, the applicant may
request a partial refund of fees, in proportion to the staff time and costs already expended, in the
Director's discretion.
