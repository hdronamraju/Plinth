# Piedmont City Code §17.48.050 Cannabis deliveries

A. State-licensed commercial cannabis facilities located outside the City may personally deliver
cannabis and cannabis products to individuals within the City, provided that such deliveries are in
strict compliance with State Cannabis Laws and have complied with Municipal Code Chapter 10
regarding business licenses.

B. Vehicles used in the delivery process must be unmarked without any designation or logo that
identifies the vehicle as a cannabis delivery vehicle.

C. Cannabis and cannabis product deliveries within the City may not occur between 9:00 p.m.
and 7:00 a.m. (Ord. 738 N.S., 12/2017)
