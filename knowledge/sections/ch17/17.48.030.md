# Piedmont City Code §17.48.030 Prohibitions on commercial cannabis facilities

Commercial cannabis facilities are prohibited in all zones in the City. No person or entity may
establish or operate a commercial cannabis facility within city limits. A property owner may not
allow its property to be used by any person or entity as a commercial cannabis facility. (Ord. 738
N.S., 12/2017; Ord. 728 N.S., 03/2017)
