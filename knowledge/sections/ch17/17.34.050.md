# Piedmont City Code §17.34.050 Completion

The applicant must complete the landscaping to the satisfaction of the Planning Director and/or
Chief Building Official. The city may require a cash deposit or letter of credit to assure
completion. If required, the city will release the financial security in increments proportionate to
the progress of landscape completion. The city may withhold final approval of a project until
landscaping is completed.
