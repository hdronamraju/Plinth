# Piedmont City Code §17.68.070 Permit to run with land

A conditional use permit granted pursuant to the provisions of this division shall run with the
land and shall continue to be valid upon a change of ownership of the business or property which
is the subject of the conditional use permit. (Ord. 783 N.S., 10/2025)
