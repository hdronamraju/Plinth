# Piedmont City Code §17.42.020 Permit requirement

A. Additional bedroom permit. Construction of not more than two additional bedrooms within
    an existing dwelling unit shall be permitted upon issuance of an additional bedroom permit
    by the Director, as provided in this Division.

B. Building permit. A building permit shall also be required for modifications of a dwelling
   unit as set forth in the California Residential Code and other building standards adopted by
   the City. (Ord. 768 N.S. 01/2023)
