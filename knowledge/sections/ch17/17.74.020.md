# Piedmont City Code §17.74.020 Application

A. Filing by owner. An application for a development agreement may only be filed by a person
having a legal or equitable interest in real property.

B. Form of application. An application for a development agreement must be on a form
approved by the Director.

C. Application fees. The applicant shall pay fees for the filing and
processing of an application as established by resolution of the City Council.
