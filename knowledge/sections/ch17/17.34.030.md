# Piedmont City Code §17.34.030 Decision

The decision-making body for the accompanying application will take action on the landscape
plan, at the same time and in the same manner as that required for the underlying application.
That body may modify the design and attach conditions as appropriate to assure compliance with
this division. The conditions may include installation of an irrigation system.

The applicant may appeal a decision on the landscape plan under division 17.78, Appeals.
