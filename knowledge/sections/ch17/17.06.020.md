# Piedmont City Code §17.06.020 Dual city lots

If an applicant seeks city approval for improvements on a lot that falls within both Piedmont and
Oakland boundaries, the city will consider the entire lot area in evaluating floor area ratio,
landscape, structure coverage and setbacks.
