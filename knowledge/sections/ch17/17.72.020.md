# Piedmont City Code §17.72.020 Initiating an amendment

A. Zoning map. An amendment to the zoning map may be initiated by motion of the City
Council or Planning Commission, or by request of all owners of property for which the change is
sought.

B. Zoning regulations. An amendment to the zoning regulations may be initiated by motion of
the City Council or the Planning Commission, or by request of a resident, property owner or
business owner in the city.

C. City Charter. A proposed amendment to the zoning map or zoning classifications is subject
to City Charter section 9.02. (See section 17.02.010C.)
