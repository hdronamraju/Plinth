# Piedmont City Code §17.48.070 Civil injunction

Any violation of this division is declared to be a public nuisance per se and contrary to the public
interest and, at the discretion of the City, will be subject to a cause of action for injunctive relief.
(Ord. 743 N.S., 05/2018; Ord. 738 N.S., 12/2017)
