# Piedmont City Code §17.68.050 Review

A. Review. Each conditional use permit is subject to review two years after the permit is
approved, or a longer period if provided in the approved permit, and each five years after that, or
longer as determined by the Planning Commission. The Director will initiate the review and
provide a report to the Planning Commission. If the Director or Planning Commission

determines that there may be grounds for revocation of the permit, the Director will schedule the
matter for a revocation hearing under section 17.80.050.

B. Applicability to prior conditional use permits. If a use permit issued before December 2013
has a condition with an automatic review or termination date, that condition is superseded by this
section.
