# Piedmont City Code §17.38.040 Permit requirement

A.      Accessory dwelling unit permit. An accessory dwelling unit permit is required for
construction of an accessory dwelling unit or junior accessory dwelling unit or the modification
of exterior features, size, or height of an existing accessory dwelling unit or junior accessory
dwelling unit.

B.     Building permit. A building permit shall be required for construction or modification of
an accessory dwelling unit or junior accessory dwelling unit as set forth in the California
Residential Code and other building standards adopted by the City. (Ord. 768 N.S., 01/2023)
