# Piedmont City Code §17.36.050 Substitution of message

A noncommercial message of any type may be substituted for all or part of the commercial or
noncommercial message on any approved sign allowed under this division. No special or
additional approval from the City is required to substitute a noncommercial message for any
other message on an allowable sign provided the sign design is already approved or exempt from
the approval requirements of this division and no design, structural, or electrical changes are
being made.
