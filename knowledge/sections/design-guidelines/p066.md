# Piedmont Design Guidelines, Page 3-31 (PDF page 66)

3.07.04 DRIVEWAY TURN AROUND STANDARDS
Driveways that exceed the maximum backup distance shall have a turnaround area
immediately adjacent to the front wall of the garage or carport.
Note: Driveways and turnarounds that do not meet these standards are not necessarily
considered “unusable” as provided in Zoning Ordinance Sec. 17.30.060.

                                                The turnaround shall be adequate in size to
                                                allow a standard vehicle one (1) two-point
                                                maneuver and an exit onto a public street in
                                                a forward direction.

                                                One (1) two-point maneuver consists of one
                                                (1) forward motion and one (1) backward
                                                motion (See the adjacent Diagram).
