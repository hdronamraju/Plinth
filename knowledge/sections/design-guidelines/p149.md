6.03.02 AESTHETIC DESIGN: ON-SITE COMPATIBILITY:
DESIGN GUIDELINES:
  1. Additions and remodels to existing multi-family dwellings should be addressed in the
     same manner outlined in Chapter 5: Zone A: Single-family Dwellings.

  2. When more than one unit is housed in the same building, the building composition
     should be read as a whole, rather than one that creates different architectural styles for
     the separate units.

  3. Provide a variety of architectural elements between buildings, to avoid repetition and
     monotony, while maintaining a unifying architectural style.
