# Piedmont Design Guidelines, Page 153

Civic Center Area:
The six lots zoned for commercial and mixed-uses fronting Highland Avenue and Highland Way
are either surrounded by public rights-of-way or adjacent to public facilities. None are directly
adjacent to lots zoned for residential use. Due to lot configurations and orientations to the
street and public buildings, their exposures may be viewed from all sides. The context of this
district is defined by the existing public and semi-public buildings within the Civic Center area,
including City Hall, Piedmont Community Church, the Veterans Building, and Havens School.
