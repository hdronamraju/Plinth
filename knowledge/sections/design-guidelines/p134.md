# Piedmont Design Guidelines, Page 5-9 (PDF page 134)

DESIGN GUIDELINE:
  2. If a detached garage has its entrance a short distance from the street, ensure that its
     design is consistent with similarly positioned structures on adjacent properties and
     contributes positively to the character of the street.

DESIGN COMMENTS:
  A. Because of topographic conditions, many residences in Piedmont have a detached
     garage whose entrance is located a short distance from the access street. Given the
     visual prominence of this type of garage, it is an important element in the overall
     character of the neighborhood. To reduce perceived mass, the preference is to use two
     independent garage doors, rather than a single wide garage door. To the extent
     permitted by topography, the siting of a new garage and its physical relationship to the
     residence should replicate other garages in the neighborhood.
