   3. The Measurement of Fences and Retaining Walls
      Ref: Zoning Ordinance Sec. 17.90.020

3.08.01 NEIGHBORHOOD COMPATIBILITY
DESIGN GUIDELINES:
  1. The design of new retaining walls that are visible from the street should be consistent
     with the scale and proportion of existing retaining walls on contiguous parcels, except
     when they exceed the recommended maximum heights outlined in these guidelines.
