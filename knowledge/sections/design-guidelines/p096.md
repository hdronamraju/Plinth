3.13.04 ON-SITE SAFETY
DESIGN GUIDELINES:
  1. Water features, including hot tubs, pools and fountains with reservoirs greater than 12
     inches in depth should be locked from public access as required by the Building Code.

  2. Heat generating equipment, such as pool heating equipment, fire pits, fire place
     openings, and cooking appliances should be a minimum of 36 inches clear from plant
     materials, unless they are rated to be in closer proximity to flammable materials.
