5.01.01 NEIGHBORHOOD AND CONTIGUOUS PARCEL
        COMPATIBILITY
DESIGN GUIDELINES:
  1. On steeply sloping properties, it is important to minimize building bulk. This is
     accomplished by respecting existing topography and following the contours of the
     existing slope. “Stepping down” with the slope reduces the building’s effective visual
     bulk and avoids the appearance of an excessively large, bulky building.
