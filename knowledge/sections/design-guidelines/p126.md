5.01 BUILDING SCALE AND MASSING
Guidelines for the scale and massing of new and expanded existing single-family structures are
influenced by multiple factors, including prevailing lot widths and depths, site access,
topography, and natural vegetation. Some settings may require greater sensitivity to existing
conditions than others. For example, in hillside neighborhoods, new structures and additions
can stand out prominently when seen from a distance or from below, since they cannot be
screened as effectively by trees and plantings.
