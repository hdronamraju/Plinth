# Piedmont Design Guidelines, Page 155

7.02 SITE DEVELOPMENT
DESIGN STANDARDS:
   1. Encourage land uses, activities, design changes, circulation changes, and capital
      improvements which transform the Piedmont Civic Center into a more cohesive
      pedestrian-oriented gathering place.
      Ref: General Plan Land Use Element Policy 4.1
   2. Create more distinctive and memorable points of entry into the city to provide a
      stronger sense of arrival and define city edges.
      Ref: General Plan Design and Preservation Element Policy 27.4
   3. Within the Grand Avenue Commercial District, Encourage Mixed-use Development
      that combines ground floor commercial uses with upper story residential uses.
      Ref: General Plan Land Use Policy 2.2
   4. Buffering and screening should be required between commercial development and
      adjacent residential properties.
      Ref: General Plan Land Use Policy 2.5
   5. Recognize commercial uses as gathering places, contributing to pedestrian vitality and
      safety.
      Ref: General Plan Land Use Policy 2.6
   6. Consider potential impacts on city and school district properties and facilities.
      Ref: General Plan Land Use Policy 3.4
   7. Zone D – Commercial/ Mixed-Use Regulations.
      Ref: Zoning Ordinance Section 17.26

7.02.01 AMENITIES IN THE STREET RIGHT-OF-WAY
DESIGN GUIDELINES: NEIGHBORHOOD COMPATIBILITY:
  1. Introduce new street trees to match the spacing of existing street trees in front of
     contiguous neighboring properties. If there are no street trees in front of contiguous
     neighboring properties, provide new street trees with a minimum spacing of 25 feet.

  2. Provide Class 2 bicycle racks for short term use, when feasible.

  3. Encourage usable public outdoor spaces with seating in front of commercial storefronts,
     when feasible.
