DESIGN GUIDELINES:
  4. The siting of a new detached or attached garage on a parcel should minimize any
     adverse impacts on the residence which it serves.

  5. The design of a new detached or attached garage should avoid compromising the
     amenities of the residence it will serve, such as converting a yard into a garage, blocking
     a view, or reducing its sense of openness.
