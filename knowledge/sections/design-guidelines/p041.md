# Piedmont Design Guidelines, Page 3-6 (PDF page 41)

                              Estates. Estate lots typically include
                              Piedmont’s grandest homes, including those
                              designed by notable architects and those
                              considered “iconic” by residents and visitors.
                              These areas include streets such as Sea View,
                              King, and Crocker Avenues, Hampton Road,
                              and the Glen Alpine-Sotelo Loop. Lots are
                              generally larger than 25,000 square feet and
                              may be more than an acre in some cases.
                              While most of these homes have street-facing
                              front facades, some are not entirely visible
                              from the street. Estate parcels often include
                              accessory structures such as pool houses or
                              guest quarters, and may include formal
                              landscapes and gardens. Piedmont has a
                              separate zoning district for its estate lots,
                              recognizing their unique character.
