# Piedmont Design Guidelines, Page 3-2 (PDF page 37)

                              Lower Piedmont. Portions of Lower
                              Piedmont, including the areas immediately
                              adjacent to the Rose Garden, Grand Lake and
                              Lakeshore districts of Oakland, are generally
                              characterized by early 20th Century bungalows
                              on lots of less than 6,000 square feet. Home
                              styles are eclectic and often vary from lot to
                              lot. Most lots are deeper than they are wide
                              with homes built close to the street. Given the
                              relatively small size of these homes, there is
                              strong demand for additions. Common design
                              issues are scale, mass, privacy, parking,
                              shadows, and view obstruction. Piedmont has
                              many examples of highly successful remodels
                              and additions on small lots that respect the
                              integrity of the original home and neighboring
                              properties.
