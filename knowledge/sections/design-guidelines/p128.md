# Piedmont Design Guidelines, Page 5-3 (PDF page 128)

DESIGN GUIDELINE:
  2. A new or expanded structure should be physically integrated into the neighborhood, so
     it appears to residents and visitors that it properly belongs there. Maintaining
     compatibility with the scale and mass of the existing buildings on contiguous parcels
     prevents the new or expanded structure from overpowering or dominating the existing
     construction within the neighborhood.
