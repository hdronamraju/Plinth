                                        Chapter 28
                        PROTECTION OF POLICE DEPARTMENT CANINES


     '28.1 Prohibited conduct
     '28.2 Penalties


     SEC. 28.1      PROHIBITED CONDUCT

             It shall be unlawful for any person to willfully or maliciously tease, taunt, torment, strike,
     kick, mutilate, disable or otherwise injure or kill, or to unwarrantably interfere or meddle with
     any canine while such animal is being used by the City of Piedmont police department, or any
     officer or other employee thereof, in the performance of any function or duty of said department
     or of such officer or employee. (Ord. No. 460 N.S., '2, eff. 8/2/84)

     SEC. 28.2      PENALTIES

             Any person violating any of the provisions of this chapter shall be guilty of a
     misdemeanor and upon conviction thereof, shall be fined in an amount not exceeding five hundred
     dollars ($500.00) or imprisoned in the county jail for a period not exceeding ninety (90) days or
     by being both so fined and imprisoned. (Ord. No. 460 N.S., '2, eff. 8/2/84)
