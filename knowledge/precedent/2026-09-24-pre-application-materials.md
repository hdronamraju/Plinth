# What to send before a pre-application meeting with a planner

## Question

Can we schedule a meeting with a planner to review preliminary variance findings and plans before submitting?

## Staff answer (verbatim)

> Please send preliminary drawings or schematics, variance findings, and any of your questions so we can assign you to the planner best suited for your project and prepare for the meeting.
