# FAR, lot coverage and landscape coverage include the entire footprint, even past the property line

## Question

The zoning ordinance has no guidance on floor area, lot coverage or FAR for structures encroaching onto another property. We propose calculating lot coverage and FAR excluding any portion over the property line. Should these values be calculated differently?

## Staff answer (verbatim)

> To clarify, the entire footprint (of all structures) and floor area of the habitable spaces must be included when calculating FAR, lot coverage, and landscape coverage, regardless of location—even for portions that extend beyond the property boundaries.

## Context

The applicant proposed excluding the portion over the property line, per the definitions in 17.90.020. Staff ruled the opposite. Staff also pointed to a chapter of the Design Guidelines (chapter not named in the email).
