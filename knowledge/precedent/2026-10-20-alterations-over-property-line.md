# No alterations to portions of a building over the property line; in-kind repairs allowed

## Question

The survey shows the house's northern side is over the property line. What are the implications for alterations?

## Staff answer (verbatim)

> Unfortunately there are no options for alterations to portions of the building existing over the property line, however, in-kind repairs would be allowed.

## Context

Answered after Planning and Building and Public Works conferred. Likely applies generally to encroaching structures; confirm before relying on it for another property.
