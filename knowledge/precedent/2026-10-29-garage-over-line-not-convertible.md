# A structure over the property line could not be converted to habitable space

## Question

If converting the existing garage (partly over the property line) to an ADU, can the roof be raised, up to 18 feet, with a loft?

## Staff answer (verbatim)

> Unfortunately, a structure situated over the property line could not be converted to habitable space. If the owners wanted to develop an ADU on the property, they could not convert the existing garage.
